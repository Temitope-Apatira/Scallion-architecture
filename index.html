01 — The Studio

We design with restraint, so that what matters can stand out.

Founded on the belief that good architecture is quietly transformative, Scanllion brings together architects, interior designers and planners who share a passion for craft. Every project begins with listening — to our clients, to the site, and to the people who will live with the building long after we're gone.

We favour honest materials, natural light and clear, generous plans. The result is architecture that feels inevitable: precise in its detail, sustainable in its performance, and timeless in its character.

  • Context first. Each building responds to its climate, culture and landscape.
  • Sustainable by design. Passive strategies and low-carbon materials as standard.
  • Crafted detail. We stay involved from first sketch to final handover.

03 — What We Do

Services

01Architecture

Full architectural services for new builds, extensions and adaptive reuse — from feasibility and concept design through planning, technical drawings and construction.

02Interior Design

Cohesive interiors that extend the architecture inward: spatial planning, joinery, lighting, material and furniture selection.

03Urban Planning

Masterplans and public-realm strategies that create walkable, mixed-use neighbourhoods with lasting community value.

04Landscape

Gardens, courtyards and landscapes designed in dialogue with the building, using native planting and sustainable water management.

05Project Management

Contract administration and site supervision to make sure the design intent is delivered on time, on budget and to the highest standard.

04 — How We Work

Our process

  1. 01

    Discover

    We study the brief, the site and your aspirations to define what success looks like.

  2. 02

    Concept

    Sketches, models and visualisations explore options and set a clear design direction.

  3. 03

    Develop

    We refine the design, secure approvals and produce detailed technical documentation.
